# Prayer Tracker - Your Islamic Prayer Companion

**Stay consistent with your daily prayers and connect with fellow Muslims worldwide.**

## 🕌 Features

### Prayer Tracking
- **Daily Prayer Logging**: Track all 5 daily prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- **Prayer Status**: Mark prayers as Jamaa'ah, Alone, Late, or Missed
- **Smart Statistics**: View detailed analytics of your prayer consistency
- **Monthly Reports**: Track your progress over time with beautiful charts

### Social Features
- **Friend System**: Connect with friends using email addresses
- **Privacy Controls**: Choose what prayer data to share with friends
- **Motivation**: See friends' progress to stay motivated
- **Community**: Build accountability with fellow Muslims

### Beautiful Design
- **Islamic Aesthetics**: Beautiful Arabic typography and Islamic design elements
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Automatic theme switching based on system preferences
- **Smooth Animations**: Delightful micro-interactions throughout the app
- **Responsive Design**: Perfect experience on all device sizes

### Data & Privacy
- **Secure Backend**: All data stored securely with Supabase
- **Privacy First**: Full control over what data you share
- **Offline Support**: Continue tracking even without internet
- **Data Export**: Your data belongs to you

## 🎯 Perfect For

- Muslims wanting to improve prayer consistency
- Parents teaching children about prayer habits
- Communities building accountability together
- Anyone seeking spiritual growth through technology

## 📱 Why Choose Prayer Tracker?

Unlike other prayer apps, Prayer Tracker focuses on **consistency and community**. We believe that tracking your prayers and connecting with others creates powerful motivation for spiritual growth.
