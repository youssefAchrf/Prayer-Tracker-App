# Privacy Policy for Prayer Tracker

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## Introduction

Prayer Tracker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, and profile picture (optional)
- **Prayer Data**: Your daily prayer tracking information and status
- **Friend Connections**: Information about your friend relationships within the app

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and app version
- **Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the app

## How We Use Your Information

- **Core Functionality**: To provide prayer tracking and social features
- **Account Management**: To maintain your account and preferences
- **Friend Features**: To connect you with friends and share permitted data
- **App Improvement**: To analyze usage and improve our services
- **Communication**: To send important updates about the app

## Data Sharing and Privacy

### What We Share
- **With Friends**: Only prayer data you explicitly choose to share
- **Service Providers**: With trusted partners who help us operate the app
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights

### What We Don't Share
- We never sell your personal information
- We don't share prayer data without your explicit consent
- We don't use your data for advertising purposes

## Your Privacy Controls

- **Friend Privacy**: Choose which friends can see your prayer data
- **Profile Privacy**: Control who can find you by email
- **Data Access**: View and download all your data anytime
- **Account Deletion**: Delete your account and all associated data

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission
- Secure cloud storage with Supabase
- Regular security audits
- Limited access to personal data

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.
